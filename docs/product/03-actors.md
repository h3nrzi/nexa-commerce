# Nexa Commerce — Actors

## Purpose

This is the initial map of people and organizations involved in Nexa's online commerce journey. An actor describes a business responsibility, not a job title or a rule about who can perform a particular action. One person may cover more than one internal role.

## Actors and responsibilities

| Actor | Responsibility in the commerce journey |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Shops online, places and pays for orders, chooses home delivery or store pickup, and raises post-purchase requests. |
| Commercial team | Decides which products Nexa offers and manages product information, prices, and promotions. |
| Inventory and warehouse team | Maintains availability at the central warehouse, prepares warehouse-supplied parts of orders, and hands parcels to the delivery company. |
| Store staff | Maintains availability at a store, prepares orders supplied by that store, and hands pickup orders to customers. |
| Customer support | Helps customers with order issues and coordinates cancellation, return, and refund requests. |
| Contracted delivery company | Collects parcels for home delivery, delivers them to customers, and provides delivery outcomes to Nexa. |

## Boundaries

- Nexa is the seller to the customer. Product brands are not sellers or independent marketplace actors.
- The central warehouse and stores can supply different parts of one order.
- Store staff participate in online order fulfillment and pickup; in-store sales are outside the current scope.
- The contracted delivery company performs home delivery; Nexa remains the customer's retail counterparty.
- Specific approval rights and operational policies for these roles will be defined later.
