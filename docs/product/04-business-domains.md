# Nexa Commerce — Business Domains

## Purpose

These domains describe the main parts of Nexa's online retail journey. They are a product-level map for deciding capabilities and business rules; their boundaries do not prescribe how Nexa organizes its teams.

| Domain | Product responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Catalog and assortment | Define which consumer electronics, gaming products, and accessories Nexa offers, with the information customers need to choose among them. Nexa is the seller of every offer. |
| Pricing and promotions | Determine the price presented to a customer and how applicable promotions affect a purchase. |
| Inventory and availability | Represent where products are available across the central warehouse and stores, and whether they can be offered for delivery or pickup. |
| Shopping and checkout | Let customers select products, review the purchase and fulfillment choices, and place an online order. |
| Orders | Maintain the customer's purchase and its progress, including orders fulfilled in multiple parts. |
| Payments | Handle payment for an order and the monetary side of any refund. |
| Fulfillment and delivery | Arrange supply from Nexa's locations, hand home-delivery parcels to the contracted delivery company, and support pickup at stores. |
| Post-purchase service | Handle cancellation and return requests, resolve their outcomes, and coordinate any resulting refund. |

## Current scope limits

- Physical store checkout is outside the online commerce journey, although stores can supply and hand over online orders.
- Independent marketplace sellers and B2B procurement are outside Nexa's model.
- Customer loyalty programs and gift cards are outside the current scope.
- Some detailed policies, including promotion eligibility, home-delivery location priority, and product-category return exceptions, remain open.
