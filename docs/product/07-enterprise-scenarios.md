# Nexa Commerce — Enterprise Scenarios

## Purpose

These examples test how the agreed product rules work together in realistic retail situations. They cover the overall product, including capabilities planned after the first release. They illustrate expected business outcomes; they do not add new policies.

## 1. One home-delivery order, multiple supply locations

A customer orders a game console and a controller for home delivery and pays online. The console is available at the central warehouse, while a Nexa store has the controller. Nexa chooses the supply locations and fulfills the order in two parts. The customer can follow each part and may receive them on different days. The customer does not choose the supplying locations.

## 2. Pickup requires stock at one store

A customer wants to collect a laptop and a mouse at one Nexa store. Pickup is available only if both items are already available at that selected store. Nexa does not move an item from the central warehouse or another store to make this pickup order possible. The customer pays online before collection.

## 3. Unexpected shortage after payment

After a home-delivery order is paid, one item is unexpectedly unavailable at its planned supply location. Nexa looks for another eligible location. If none can supply it, Nexa cancels only that item, refunds its paid amount to the original payment method, and continues fulfilling the remaining items. Their prices and discounts stay as agreed at purchase.

## 4. Partial cancellation as fulfillment progresses

A two-part home-delivery order has one parcel already handed to the delivery company and another still with Nexa. The customer can cancel the part still with Nexa and receive the amount paid for it. The handed-over part continues; if the customer later receives it and wants to send it back, the return policy applies.

## 5. Return through a different channel

A customer receives headphones at home and decides to return them within 30 days. If the item is in good condition and complete, the customer can take it to any Nexa store or use the contracted delivery company. An accepted return is refunded through the original online payment method. The prices of other items in the original order do not change.

## 6. Guest purchase and after-sales service

A customer checks out with a mobile number and without creating an account. Later, the customer uses the order number and a verification code sent to that mobile number to follow the order's parts and request cancellation or return under the same rules as a customer with an account. Account creation is not a condition of purchase or after-sales service.

## 7. Pickup order not collected

A customer pays online for store pickup, and the whole order becomes ready at the selected store. If the customer does not collect it within three calendar days, Nexa cancels the order, releases its reserved stock, and refunds the payment to the original card or through the agreed refund fallback if needed.

If the original-card refund fails, customer support matches the request to the order and payment and verifies the original cardholder and that person's ownership of the destination bank account. The refund stays pending until those checks pass and the alternative payment succeeds.

## 8. Customer unavailable for home delivery

One part of a home-delivery order reaches the customer's address, but the customer is unavailable. The contracted delivery company coordinates one more attempt with the customer. If the customer is still unavailable, the parcel returns to Nexa. Nexa cancels that part and refunds the amount paid for its items. Any other order parts continue, and the order's fixed delivery charge remains.

## 9. Parcel lost or damaged during delivery

The delivery company reports that a parcel was lost or damaged before reaching the customer. Nexa supplies a replacement from eligible stock without another charge. If no replacement is available, Nexa refunds the amount paid for its items. Other parts of the order continue. If all parcels are lost or damaged, no replacement can be supplied, and nothing from the order is delivered, Nexa also refunds the fixed delivery charge.
